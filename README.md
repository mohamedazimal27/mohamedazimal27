## About Me

Automation & robotics engineer with **8+ years** designing, programming, and commissioning industrial systems across semiconductor, medical device, logistics, and automotive plants in Singapore, Malaysia, and India.

I specialize in **Beckhoff TwinCAT 3 / EtherCAT motion control**, **FANUC/ABB robot integration**, **MiR AGV/AMR fleet management**, and **Cognex/iRVision machine vision** — and I'm now extending that into **ROS 2 and modern robotics software** (Nav2, SLAM, Gazebo).

- 🌱 Learning: ROS 2 actions, Nav2 tuning, and PLC ↔ ROS integration
